# Terms

Public route: `/terms`.

Rồng Con Du Ký is an educational hackathon demo, not a booking service, emergency service or official travel/safety guidance. Landmark facts and Starter recommendations are authored, sourced content; they are not live operational data. Check official channels for weather, opening conditions, prices, routes and safety requirements before travelling.

The Sơn Trà quest is an observation message: never chase, touch, feed or disturb wildlife. Follow local signage and permitted routes.

Google Maps opens as an external service and third-party terms apply. Bundled art is a team-owned placeholder set; final public release requires rights approval for every replacement/additional asset. The MVP can fall back locally if Firebase, Gemini or the backend is unavailable and makes no availability or real-time-accuracy guarantee.
